In general, and especially for our Open Source Common Ground projects, we prefer a [European sovereign cloud provider](/platforms-and-operations/european-sovereign-cloud) over the US hyperscalers.

Depending on the context and specific project requirements, we can still opt for a US hyperscaler, with [AWS](/platforms-and-operations/aws) our preferred choice there. Azure remains in the adopt ring as well: we see continued traction in our market and get requests from clients to keep actively supporting it, but our preference for Azure is lower than for AWS.

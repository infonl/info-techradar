In general, and especially for our Open Source Common Ground projects, we prefer a [European sovereign cloud provider](/platforms-and-operations/european-sovereign-cloud) over the US hyperscalers, to keep data and operations within EU jurisdiction.

Depending on the context and specific project requirements, we can still opt for a US hyperscaler. When we do, [AWS](https://aws.amazon.com) is our preferred choice: it is the (local as well as global) market leader in cloud-native computing and offers the best set of services for our needs when compared to [Azure](/platforms-and-operations/azure).
